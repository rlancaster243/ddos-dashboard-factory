# Executive Command Center

## Visual posture
Near-black or deep-graphite backgrounds. Compact KPI rows. Restrained accents — usually one warm or steel-blue accent doing all the work. Type is tight, modern sans (Inter, IBM Plex Sans, Geist). No decorative gradients. Whitespace is the luxury.

## When to choose
- Executive review meetings, monthly business reviews, board packets
- The reader has 30 seconds and wants the call, not the story
- The dashboard supports a *decision*, not exploration

## When NOT to choose
- Long-form analytical narrative (use Editorial Intelligence Brief)
- Real-time ops with severity coloring (use Technical Operations Console)
- Bright/marketing-friendly views (use Modern SaaS Analytics)

## Themes in this archetype
- `ecc-midnight-graphite` — Steel-blue accent on graphite. Pure executive minimalism.
- `ecc-deep-navy-amber` — Deep navy with warm amber accent. Carries warmth; matches an existing globe-explorer palette.
