# Institutional Enterprise

## Visual posture
Steel-gray or warm-stone backgrounds. Conservative serif or stable sans (Source Sans 3, Atkinson Hyperlegible). Navy or forest-green accent. Strong table styling. WCAG AA contrast minimum. Looks like it could ship to a federal agency or a tier-1 bank without flinching.

## When to choose
- Regulator-facing reports, audit committees, compliance
- Multi-stakeholder views where personality must be neutral
- Accessibility-required deployments

## When NOT to choose
- Anything where personality or warmth matters (use Editorial or SaaS)
- Real-time ops (use Tech Ops Console)

## Themes in this archetype
- `ie-corporate-steel` — Steel gray, navy accent. Banking/government default.
- `ie-stable-stone` — Warm stone, forest green accent. Established, conservative.
