# Editorial Intelligence Brief

## Visual posture
Warm off-white or newsprint backgrounds. Serif display type (Fraunces, Newsreader) paired with a clean sans body (Source Sans 3, Plus Jakarta). Headlines act as section markers. Color sparse — used for emphasis only. Reads like a long-form intelligence brief or *Economist* page.

## When to choose
- Weekly/monthly strategy briefs, country reports, policy analysis
- Reader will spend 3–10 minutes reading top-to-bottom
- Decision implication earned through narrative, not shouted

## When NOT to choose
- Quick KPI scan (use Executive Command Center)
- Bright marketing or product UI (use Modern SaaS Analytics)

## Themes in this archetype
- `eib-warm-parchment` — Warm parchment, walnut accent, Fraunces display.
- `eib-newsprint-ink` — Cream newsprint with ink-black serif. Classic broadsheet.
