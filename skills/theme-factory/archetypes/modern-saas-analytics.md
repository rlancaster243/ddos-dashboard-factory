# Modern SaaS Analytics

## Visual posture
Cool slate or near-white backgrounds. Refined card surfaces with subtle elevation. Indigo or electric-blue accent. Modern geometric sans (Inter, Geist, DM Sans). Looks like 2025-era product analytics: Linear, Vercel, Stripe Atlas.

## When to choose
- Internal product/growth dashboards
- The dashboard *is* a product UI users return to daily
- Reader expects interaction polish (hover states, smooth transitions)

## When NOT to choose
- Executive packet (too friendly — use ECC)
- Narrative analysis (use Editorial)

## Themes in this archetype
- `msa-cool-slate` — Cool slate dark, indigo accent.
- `msa-snowfield` — Light snow background, electric blue. Bright, modern.
