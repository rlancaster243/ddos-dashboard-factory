# Luxe Minimal

## Visual posture
Maximum whitespace, refined neutral palettes (warm off-whites, espresso darks), one carefully-chosen accent (gold jewel, dusty sage). Low `--radius` (6-14px) for sharp editorial precision. Serif or humanist display fonts (Playfair, Fraunces) paired with modern sans body text. This is editorial luxury — restraint over decoration, calm over energy.

## When to choose
- High-end internal dashboards where calm is a feature
- Wellness, people-ops, employee experience contexts
- Dashboards meant to be *read*, not scanned — narrative over density
- Contexts where refined taste signals quality (luxury brands, editorial content)

## When NOT to choose
- Real-time operations requiring fast scanning (use Technical Operations Console)
- Vibrant product/marketing contexts (use Vibrant Product Studio)
- Dense KPI grids where whitespace is a liability (use Executive Command Center)

## Themes in this archetype
- `lux-espresso-gold` — Warm espresso dark with gold jewel accent. Serif display, refined luxury.
- `lux-pastel-calm` — Warm off-white with sage and rose pastels. Humanist serif, soft calm.
