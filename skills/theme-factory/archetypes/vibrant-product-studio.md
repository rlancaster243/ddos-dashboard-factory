# Vibrant Product Studio

## Visual posture
Bold saturated primaries (teal, coral, indigo) with dual-accent gradients. Rounded cards (16px radius), generous whitespace between elements, modern sans-serif (Inter, Geist). Colorful semantic indicators. Feels energetic without being playful — this is product-studio energy, not consumer-app whimsy.

## When to choose
- Product dashboards, growth metrics, marketing performance
- User-facing analytics that need to feel modern and approachable
- Dashboard as a *feature* — when the dashboard itself is part of the product experience
- Teams that value design-forward tooling

## When NOT to choose
- High-stakes executive review (use Executive Command Center)
- Technical operations monitoring (use Technical Operations Console)
- Calm/wellness contexts where vibrant colors feel jarring (use Luxe Minimal pastel)

## Themes in this archetype
- `vps-electric-teal` — Deep teal-emerald base with electric cyan gradient. Dark mode, high energy.
- `vps-citrus-daylight` — Warm coral and teal on light base. Daylight mode, approachable warmth.
