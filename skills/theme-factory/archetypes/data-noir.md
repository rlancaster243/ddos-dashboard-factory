# Data Noir

## Visual posture
Obsidian or pure-black backgrounds. High-contrast type. One dramatic accent — crimson, electric violet, or hazard yellow. Visual weight concentrates on outliers and anomalies. Reads investigative: "what's wrong here, and where did it come from?"

## When to choose
- Risk dashboards, fraud, anomaly detection
- Investigation tooling where outliers need to scream
- Demo/showcase view that needs to feel cinematic

## When NOT to choose
- Anything board- or external-facing where neutrality matters
- High-information density needing many semantic colors (use Tech Ops)

## Themes in this archetype
- `noir-obsidian-crimson` — Obsidian black, crimson accent.
- `noir-blackout-violet` — Pure blackout with electric violet.
