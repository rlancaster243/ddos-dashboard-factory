# Glass & Aurora

## Visual posture
Frosted glass panels with backdrop blur (16-18px), translucent overlays, soft aurora gradients radiating from corners. High `--radius` (20px) for soft rounded corners. Subtle radial gradients wash the background without overwhelming content. Modern sans-serif (Inter, Geist). This is glassmorphism done with restraint — luminous, not loud.

## When to choose
- Premium product showcases, modern executive dashboards
- Public-facing analytics meant to impress (sales decks, investor presentations)
- Contexts where visual sophistication is part of the brand signal
- When you want to differentiate from flat Material/bootstrap aesthetics

## When NOT to choose
- High-density technical consoles (blur reduces scan speed)
- Text-heavy dashboards (glass layers reduce contrast, harder to read)
- Contexts where accessibility/legibility is paramount over aesthetics
- Older browsers or devices that don't support backdrop-filter

## Themes in this archetype
- `gla-aurora-nebula` — Teal-violet aurora on near-black. Glassmorphism dark mode.
- `gla-frost-daybreak` — Indigo-pink frost on pale blue. Glassmorphism light mode.
